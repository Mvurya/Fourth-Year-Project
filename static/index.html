What should my data include?

Columns: order_date, sales, discount, shipping_cost, revenue, expenses. Dates: YYYY-MM-DD. Discounts: 0–1. Revenue = sales × (1 − discount) + shipping cost. Include at least 30 consecutive months. The format example shows the columns; it is too short to train a model.

Understand the model & scores

Ordinary least squares uses trend, seasonal sine/cosine, previous-month cashflow, and same-month-last-year cashflow. Evaluation trains on earlier months, then tests on the most recent 6–12 months. One-month evaluation uses observed lags; recursive evaluation feeds its own predictions back into lags. The final model retrains on all eligible history.

MAE and RMSE measure error in currency units; lower is better. WAPE measures total absolute error relative to absolute actuals; lower is better. R² measures fit and can be negative. These are forecasting scores, not creditworthiness scores or guaranteed accuracy.
